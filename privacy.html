Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 12, 2026

Aeonix Ascend ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data when you use our SMS-based AI business coaching service.

1. Data We Collect

When you sign up or use Aeonix Ascend, we may collect the following information:

  • Your first name
  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your business type and general business information you share during coaching sessions
  • SMS message content exchanged with our coaching service
  • Basic usage data (e.g., message timestamps)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely to provide and improve your coaching experience:

  • To deliver personalized AI-powered business coaching via SMS
  • To send service updates, onboarding messages, and account notifications
  • To improve the quality and relevance of coaching responses
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. SMS Messaging Consent

By providing your phone number and submitting our opt-in form, you expressly consent to receive SMS messages from Aeonix Ascend. These messages may include coaching content, business insights, and service updates related to your subscription.

Message frequency varies based on your usage and subscription plan. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.

Your consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

4. Opt-Out Instructions

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. After opting out, you will receive a single confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent.

To re-enable messages after opting out, reply START at any time.

You may also request deletion of all your personal data by replying DELETE or by contacting us directly at the email below.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. All messages are transmitted and stored using industry-standard security practices.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
